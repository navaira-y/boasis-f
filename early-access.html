What the portal does.

A self-service tool for founders and freelancers who run 1 to 3 free zone companies in the UAE. It does four things.

KnowHolds one company file: the licence, owners and managers, office, cards, tax details. Uses it to work out everything that applies.
TellEvery date, rule and step that applies to this company, in time, by email, to the right person.
GuidePlain explanations and step-by-step instructions for each requirement, specific to the company's free zone.
KeepStores every document with its dates, so it is ready when the bank, auditor, agent or zone asks.

One pattern for every requirement.

  1. KnowledgeWhat it is, and why it matters.
  2. ChoiceWhat you want to do.
  3. StepsExactly what to do, and who does it.
  4. EvidenceProof it is done.

Nothing is saved until you confirm each value. Progress is saved after every step, and a skipped line becomes a task, not a dead end.

What it will ask you.

Under 10 minutes for a solo founder with the licence, passport and visa at hand. Every step can be finished later.

  1. Create your accountName, email, password, and the plan you are on.
  2. Add a companyFree zone or mainland, and your role in it.
  3. Your licenceFacts, status, dates, incorporation date, agent.
  4. PeopleOwners and managers, visa sponsor, passport, visa, Emirates ID.
  5. OfficeType, lease end, and whether it renews with the licence.
  6. Card and quotaEstablishment card expiry, visas allowed and used.
  7. Corporate taxRegistered? First period, year end, free zone status.
  8. VATRegistered? Period, or the two threshold questions.
  9. Your yearThe next three dates, the full year, what is still needed, reminders.
  10. EmployeesOptional, after the year: visa dates only.

Three things it will never do.

Never submits

Nothing is submitted to government or a zone. You or your agent does.

Never a fine amount

No fines are shown or calculated. Consequences are explained in words with the official source.

Never guesses

If a rule has no source, it says unknown, and says who can answer.
