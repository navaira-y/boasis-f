Important noticeThis Privacy Policy explains how Boasis FZC collects, uses, discloses and protects personal data in connection with the website boasis.ae. It applies to the Website only. Any use of a Boasis product or service will be subject to a separate privacy notice or data processing agreement.

1Introduction

1.1

This privacy policy (the "Policy") sets out the manner in which Boasis FZC, licensed under licence number 11618, with its registered office at Block B-B58-078, SRTIP, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates ("Boasis"), collects and processes Personal Data relating to persons who access or use the website located at www.boasis.ae (the "Website").

1.2

This Policy is published in accordance with Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data and its implementing instruments (the "Data Protection Law"), and forms an integral part of the Terms of Use of the Website.

1.3

By accessing or using the Website, or by submitting any form published on it, the User acknowledges that it has read and understood this Policy.

2Definitions and Interpretation

2.1

In this Policy, unless the context otherwise requires, the following expressions shall have the following meanings:

"Applicable Law" means all laws, decrees, regulations, resolutions and other legislative instruments in force from time to time in the United Arab Emirates, whether at federal level or at the level of any Emirate;

"Data Subject" means the natural person to whom the Personal Data relates;

"Personal Data" means any data relating to an identified natural person, or to a natural person who can be identified, directly or indirectly, by reference to such data;

"Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Data, including its collection, recording, organisation, storage, use, disclosure, transfer, erasure or destruction;

"Submission" means any information, data or material transmitted by a User to Boasis through the Website, including by means of the contact form or the waiting list registration form; and

"User" means any person who accesses or uses the Website.

2.2

Clause headings are inserted for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation of this Policy. Any reference to a law or legislative instrument is a reference to it as amended, extended, consolidated, re-enacted or replaced from time to time.

3Controller of the Personal Data

3.1

Boasis is the controller of the Personal Data processed in connection with the Website and determines the purposes and means of such Processing.

3.2

Any question relating to this Policy, and any request relating to Personal Data, may be addressed to Boasis by electronic mail at support@boasis.ae, or in writing at the address set out in Clause 1.1.

4Personal Data Collected

4.1

Boasis collects the following categories of Personal Data:

  1. (a)Data submitted through the contact form, namely the name, electronic mail address and telephone number of the User, the subject of the enquiry and the content of the message, together with the name of the company or authority on behalf of which the enquiry is made, where provided;
  2. (b)Data submitted through the waiting list registration form, namely the name and electronic mail address of the User and the plan in respect of which the User has expressed an interest;
  3. (c)Data contained in any correspondence exchanged with Boasis, including electronic mail and records of any demonstration requested or attended; and
  4. (d)Technical data generated automatically when the Website is accessed, namely the internet protocol (IP) address, the type and version of the browser and operating system, the pages visited, the date and time of access and, where applicable, the website from which the User was referred.
4.2

The fields marked as mandatory in a form must be completed in order for the form to be submitted. The provision of any other Personal Data is voluntary.

4.3

Where a User provides Personal Data relating to another natural person, that User represents that it is authorised to do so and has informed that person of this Policy.

5Purposes and Legal Basis of Processing

5.1

Boasis processes Personal Data for the following purposes:

  1. (a)to respond to enquiries submitted through the Website and to communicate with the User in connection with them, on the basis of the consent of the Data Subject and of the steps taken at its request;
  2. (b)to maintain the waiting list and to inform registered Users when access to a product may become available, on the basis of the consent of the Data Subject;
  3. (c)to arrange and conduct demonstrations requested by a User, on the basis of the steps taken at the request of the Data Subject;
  4. (d)to operate, maintain and secure the Website, to prevent fraud and automated or abusive submissions, and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, on the basis of the legitimate interests of Boasis;
  5. (e)to send marketing communications relating to the products of Boasis, on the basis of the consent of the Data Subject, which may be withdrawn at any time; and
  6. (f)to comply with any obligation imposed on Boasis under Applicable Law or by any competent authority.
5.2

Personal Data shall not be processed for any purpose which is incompatible with those set out in Clause 5.1 without the prior information of the Data Subject and, where required under the Data Protection Law, its consent.

6Cookies and Similar Technologies

6.1

The Website does not use cookies for advertising purposes and does not profile Users for the purpose of targeted advertising.

6.2

The Website records in the local storage of the User's browser a technical marker indicating that the introductory sequence presented on the home page has already been viewed, so that it is not displayed again on subsequent visits. That marker is stored on the device of the User, is not transmitted to Boasis and may be deleted at any time by clearing the browsing data of the browser.

6.3

The Website may use other technologies which are strictly necessary for its operation and security, and may load fonts and similar resources from third party providers. Where such a resource is loaded, the internet protocol (IP) address of the User and the technical data referred to in Clause 4.1(d) may be transmitted to the provider concerned.

6.4

The User may configure its browser to restrict or delete such technologies, in which case certain parts of the Website may not function as intended.

7Disclosure of Personal Data

7.1

Boasis does not sell, rent or trade Personal Data.

7.2

Boasis may disclose Personal Data to:

  1. (a)its service providers acting on its instructions, in particular providers of hosting, infrastructure, electronic mail, customer relationship management and technical support services, each of which is bound by contractual obligations of confidentiality and security;
  2. (b)its professional advisers, including legal, accounting and audit advisers, where necessary for the purposes set out in this Policy;
  3. (c)any competent authority, court or regulator, where such disclosure is required under Applicable Law or is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims; and
  4. (d)any person to whom Boasis transfers all or part of its business or assets, in which case Personal Data shall continue to be processed in accordance with this Policy.

8Transfers Outside the United Arab Emirates

8.1

Certain service providers referred to in Clause 7.2 may be established, or may store or process Personal Data, outside the United Arab Emirates.

8.2

Any such transfer shall be effected in accordance with the Data Protection Law, and in particular to a jurisdiction which affords an adequate level of protection or, failing that, pursuant to contractual undertakings providing appropriate safeguards or to another lawful ground for transfer.

9Retention of Personal Data

9.1

Personal Data shall be retained only for as long as is necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, and in particular:

  1. (a)data submitted through the contact form, for the period necessary to deal with the enquiry and to maintain the commercial relationship arising from it;
  2. (b)data submitted through the waiting list registration form, until access to the product concerned is made available to the User or until the User requests its removal from the list, whichever occurs first; and
  3. (c)technical data, for the period necessary for the operation and security of the Website and, where applicable, for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
9.2

Personal Data may be retained for a longer period where its retention is required under Applicable Law. Upon expiry of the applicable period, Personal Data shall be deleted or anonymised.

10Security

10.1

Boasis implements appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect Personal Data against unauthorised or unlawful access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction, including the encryption of data in transit and the restriction of access to Personal Data to those persons who require it for the purposes set out in this Policy.

10.2

No transmission of data over the internet can be guaranteed to be entirely secure, and any transmission is made at the risk of the User.

10.3

In the event of a breach of Personal Data which is likely to prejudice the privacy, confidentiality or security of a Data Subject, Boasis shall notify the competent authority and, where required under the Data Protection Law, the Data Subject concerned.

11Rights of Data Subjects

11.1

Subject to the conditions and exceptions provided under the Data Protection Law, a Data Subject is entitled:

  1. (a)to be informed of, and to obtain access to, the Personal Data concerning it which is processed by Boasis;
  2. (b)to request the rectification of inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data;
  3. (c)to request the erasure of its Personal Data;
  4. (d)to request the restriction of the Processing or to object to it, including to Processing carried out on the basis of the legitimate interests of Boasis;
  5. (e)to receive the Personal Data which it has provided in a structured and machine-readable format, and to request its transmission to another controller where technically feasible; and
  6. (f)to withdraw its consent at any time, without prejudice to the lawfulness of the Processing carried out before such withdrawal.
11.2

Any request under Clause 11.1 may be addressed to support@boasis.ae. Boasis may request such information as is reasonably necessary to verify the identity of the applicant, and shall respond within the period prescribed by the Data Protection Law and in any event within thirty (30) days of receipt of the request.

11.3

A Data Subject who considers that its rights have not been respected may lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority in the United Arab Emirates.

12Marketing Communications

12.1

Boasis shall send marketing communications only in accordance with Applicable Law, and each such communication shall contain the means by which the recipient may object to receiving further communications.

12.2

The withdrawal of consent to marketing communications shall not affect communications which are necessary to respond to an enquiry or to inform the User of a matter relating to the Website or to this Policy.

13Minors

13.1

The Website is not directed at persons under eighteen (18) years of age, and Boasis does not knowingly collect Personal Data relating to such persons. Where Boasis becomes aware that it holds such data, it shall delete it without undue delay.

14Third Party Websites

14.1

The Website may contain links to websites operated by third parties. This Policy does not apply to such websites, and Boasis accepts no responsibility for their privacy practices. Users are invited to consult the privacy policy of any such website before submitting any Personal Data to it.

15Automated Decision-Making

15.1

Boasis does not take, by means of the Website, any decision based solely on the automated Processing of Personal Data which produces legal effects concerning a Data Subject or which significantly affects it.

16Amendments to this Policy

16.1

Boasis may amend this Policy at any time by publishing a revised version on the Website indicating the date of its latest update. Any such amendment shall take effect from the date of its publication. Users are advised to review this Policy periodically.

17Governing Law

17.1

This Policy shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the United Arab Emirates, and Clause 18 of the Terms of Use shall apply to any dispute arising out of or in connection with it.

18Contact

18.1

Any enquiry regarding this Policy or the Processing of Personal Data by Boasis may be addressed to Boasis FZC, Block B-B58-078, SRTIP, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates, at support@boasis.ae.
