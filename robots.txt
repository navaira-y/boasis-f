# BOASIS · boasis.ae · what the crawlers may take
#
# Everything a visitor can read, a crawler may read. There is nothing private on the public
# side of this site: no member area, no staging copy, no search results. The API is the one
# place a crawler has no business, and it is closed here as a second lock — lib/protect.js
# already answers every one of those paths with a 404 before it reaches a route.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /api/

# The AI assistants and answer engines, named on purpose. This site wants to be read, quoted
# and cited by them, so the answer is spelled out rather than left to a wildcard: an unnamed
# bot operator reads `User-agent: *` as "undecided" and throttles. Google-Extended and
# Applebot-Extended are the training switches, not the search ones: allowing them does not
# change where boasis.ae ranks in Search, and disallowing them would only remove BOASIS from
# the answers. docs/SEO.md says how to flip either one.
User-agent: GPTBot
User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
User-agent: ChatGPT-User
User-agent: ClaudeBot
User-agent: Claude-User
User-agent: anthropic-ai
User-agent: PerplexityBot
User-agent: Google-Extended
User-agent: Applebot-Extended
User-agent: Bingbot
Allow: /
Disallow: /api/
